# 14.4.6 — Consistent Call confidence and decision path

## Repairs

- The legacy quality-to-lean adapter expresses DOWN confidence as100−P(UP),
  including genuine zero inputs. Invalid/missing probabilities are not neutral
  fabricated reads. The adapter now respects the existingNO_SITOUT_MODE=false:
  failed quality checks are waits, not qualified candidates. Correcting arithmetic
  must not grant permission to bypass the original quality rejection.
- Older entry-quality/extreme, conviction-scaled price-window and negative-edge
  checks honor the existingNO_ENTRY_GATES switch. They no longer veto a qualified
  in-band candidate while their configuration says they are disabled. Numeric
  thresholds and enable flags are unchanged; the legacy filters still work when
  explicitly enabled. User-opted coin-flip/Tier1/loss-cooldown preferences remain.
- The intentional, independently requested44–77c Call cost band is NOT removed
  by the legacy no-entry-filter mode. Its existing enabled/override settings and
  inclusive bounds remain. Candidate filtering and timing now read the same band;
  a qualified74c candidate can reach the normal commit path without the legacy69c
  ceiling, while an out-of-band90c candidate waits. AutoTrade's separate band
  readers and execution path are unchanged.
- A retained claimed direction waits if the current candidate points the other
  way, instead of committing the claim with the opposite candidate's confidence
  and quality. Direction/stability requirements and sample weighting are not
  relaxed. The actual direction-stability and flat-gap signal waits are recorded
  explicitly, with the unchanged8-point gap/external-signal requirements.
- Timing comments no longer promise a late15m rescue: the authoritative7-minute
  cutoff runs first. The inherited late/final logic remains available for5m Calls
  under their unchanged30-second cutoff, not for new late15m decisions.
